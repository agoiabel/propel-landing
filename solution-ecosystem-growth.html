Recurring programmes

Plan the next workshop, project, product challenge or feedback cycle around what members need and what your business wants to achieve. Community leaders and programme support connect those activities into a clear rhythm.

What a programme can include

  • ✓Community listening and audience mapping.
  • ✓Workshops, learning cohorts and practical challenges.
  • ✓Ambassador and champion participation.
  • ✓Feedback and research cycles.
  • ✓Project showcases and recognition.
  • ✓Connections to work, partnership and implementation opportunities.

After the event

After a workshop, give people a task they can complete. After a hackathon, make room for project review and follow-on activity. After onboarding, help members find a useful way to contribute.

Each next step is designed into the programme, with responsibilities and measures agreed in advance.

What you receive

An audience and community plan, an agreed activity calendar, participant coordination, completed programme outputs and a report for each cycle. Reporting covers participation, completed actions, community feedback and return participation where it can be tracked.

The next cycle is planned around what the work and participant feedback show.
