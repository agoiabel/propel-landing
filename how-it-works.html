Our process

How it works

Propel recruits the people your brief needs, supports their participation and reviews the work. Where the engagement continues, we measure ongoing activity. Community leaders provide a trusted route to participants; programme and local partners support the work where needed.

Discuss your brief

Your goal

Who do you need to reach? What should they do? What would count as a useful result?

A course completion, a project submission, a product test and a specialist hire each need a different delivery plan.

How a programme runs

One process for every engagement. The final stage changes with the work: a research brief may end with accepted outputs, while a learning or adoption programme continues.

  1. 1

    Define the outcome

    Agree who you need, what they should do and how success will be measured.

  2. 2

    Recruit and qualify

    Map relevant communities, then confirm identity, skills and experience against the brief.

  3. 3

    Support participation

    Onboarding, learning, technical sessions and day-to-day coordination while the work runs.

  4. 4

    Review the work

    Check submissions, data and feedback against the standard your team sets.

  5. 5

    Continue and measure

    Accepted outputs for research. Follow-up support and agreed usage tracking for everything else.

Our delivery team

Communities and their leaders

The relationships that connect the brief to people with relevant interests, experience and skills.

Learning partners

Curriculum, facilitation and assessment support where the programme involves skill development.

Infrastructure partners and local operators

The practical arrangements that make participation and delivery possible in each market.

Propel's platform and delivery team

Participant coordination, programme workflows and records of progress and work delivered.

The mix changes with the brief. Propel's role is to make those parts work together.

Local delivery

How we deliver locally.

We keep what we have delivered, where we have relationships and what we would assess first clearly separate, so you can judge what we can run in your market.

  • Completed delivery

    Mastercard Foundation in Nigeria and Kenya. Hedera across 20+ African cities. Google AI Skills Training in Portugal.

  • Established community relationships

    Specialised communities across 30 countries in the Propel ecosystem. Relationships are not the same as completed programmes.

  • Other markets

    Assessed on request. Tell us the market and the audience, and we will say what looks realistic before you commit.

What you bring

Your objective, audience requirements, product or programme knowledge, and the access participants need to take part. For learning programmes, that may include curriculum. For testing, it includes the product and scenarios to explore.

What Propel delivers

A scoped programme, participant recruitment, coordination and support, agreed outputs and reporting. Roles, approvals and responsibilities are set before the programme begins.

Who does what

Responsibilities are agreed per brief

  1. Your team Objective, content/access and review criteria → Brief and access
  2. Propel Recruitment, coordination, programme support and delivery records → Programme coordination
  3. Community partners A relevant route to participants → Participant recruitment
  4. Your team + Propel Review the agreed work and decide next steps → Outputs and reporting
