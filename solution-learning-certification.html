Learning & certification

You have the curriculum and the goal. Propel recruits the right learners and supports them through cohorts, facilitation and practical activities, with progress measured against your programme objectives.

Scope a learning programme

Learning support

People need a reason to start, support when they get stuck and a clear next step. Community leaders, facilitators and peers help make that participation possible.

We organise those parts around your content, from the first invitation through completion and application.

What we do

  • ✓Recruit learners aligned to your audience and eligibility requirements.
  • ✓Organise cohorts, onboarding and programme communications.
  • ✓Coordinate live facilitation, peer support and progress checkpoints.
  • ✓Support assessments, certification pathways and applied work where included.
  • ✓Report participation, completion and the agreed programme outcomes.

After the course

The next step can be a project, a product workflow, a certification or an income opportunity. We design that pathway with you, so the programme has a purpose beyond its final lesson.

Your content and our delivery

Your team or learning partner owns the curriculum and learning standards. Propel coordinates recruitment, participant support and delivery. Assessment, certification and any content changes are agreed with the programme owner.

What we measure

Recruitment and enrolment tell you who entered. Participation and completion show how people moved through the programme. Assessment, certification and applied outputs show what they achieved.

Where product use or a work pathway is part of the brief, we agree how those outcomes will be followed and reported.
