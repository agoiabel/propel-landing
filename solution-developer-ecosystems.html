Sign-ups are not the goal. The programme is built around what you want developers to produce, and whether they keep building once it ends.

What Propel runs

Your team sets the technical priorities, resources and review standards. Propel runs the people and the programme around them.

What happens afterwards

A programme should not end at the submission deadline. Selected teams receive further support to keep building, and activity is measured over a window agreed before launch.

  1. 1SelectionYour team and Propel choose the projects worth taking further.
  2. 2Follow-on supportTechnical sessions, mentoring and project support for selected teams.
  3. 3Measured windowContinued activity is tracked against the measures you agreed.

What gets reported

We report the stages agreed for your programme. Project submissions are reported as submissions, and continued usage is reported only where it can be measured.
