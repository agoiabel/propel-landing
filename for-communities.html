How to get involved

What leaders bring

You know your community: its interests, experience and the way people participate. That knowledge helps us shape invitations and programmes that fit.

Propel brings enterprise briefs, programme coordination and participant support. For applicable engagements, revenue-sharing arrangements are agreed with the community partner.

  1. Community leader

    Your members' context, interests and the way they participate.

  2. Propel

    Enterprise opportunities, programme coordination and participant support.

  3. Members

    Opted-in participation in opportunities that match their interests.

  4. Together

    Feedback that shapes the next opportunity.

What your members can access

Learning with a purpose

Supported programmes, technical learning and certification pathways connected to real enterprise goals.

Work they can apply their skills to

Product tests, research tasks, developer challenges, projects and relevant hiring opportunities.

Ways to be seen

Practical outputs and participation can help members demonstrate their capabilities.

Support for professional growth

Opportunities, community resources and selected partner benefits. Device financing and other financial services depend on market availability and eligibility.

Participation requirements

Members should know the work, time commitment, selection criteria and any payment terms before they take part. Each opportunity has its own requirements.
