2. What we process and why

Visiting the website

When you load a page, our hosting provider receives technical data such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the browser and device type, and the referring page. We use it to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix faults. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a stable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Server logs are kept only for a short period.

Contact and brief form

If you send a brief, we process what you enter: your name, work email, organisation, what you are planning, how the work will run and any details you add about audience, markets, timing and outcomes. We use it to answer your enquiry, scope a possible programme and follow up. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Submissions are passed through the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards them to our mailbox.

Email and other correspondence

If you write to us, we process your message and contact details to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR, depending on whether you are asking about a contract or a general question.

Community leaders

If you contact us about a community partnership, we process the details you give us about yourself and your community on the same basis and for the same purpose: to respond and to explore working together.

You are not required to give us any of this data, but we cannot answer an enquiry without a way to reach you. We do not make decisions about you by automated means, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Cookies and third-party content

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add them, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

Some resources, such as icon files, may be loaded from content delivery networks. Their operators receive your IP address as part of the request. Links to LinkedIn, Instagram and X take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

4. Who receives data

Only those who need it to run the website and handle enquiries: our website hosting provider, the form service (FormSubmit), our email and productivity providers, and IT and professional advisers bound by confidentiality. Where a provider processes data on our behalf, we use a data processing agreement. We share data with public authorities only where the law requires it.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers, including the form service and parts of our hosting and email infrastructure, may process data in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, we rely on an adequacy decision or on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 44 ff. GDPR). You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we use.

6. How long we keep data

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to answer them and to follow up, and generally delete them within 24 months of our last contact. If we enter into a contract, we keep the records the law requires, for example under German commercial and tax law, for the statutory period. Server logs are deleted after a short period.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data erased (Art. 17)
  • restrict how we use your data (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting earlier processing (Art. 7(3))

To use any of these rights, email hello@propel.io. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. The authority responsible for us is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany. You may also contact the authority in your own country.

8. Security, children and changes

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections to this website. This website is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data. We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
